💡 Optimisation Tips

📏Google truncates by pixel width, not character count. A 55-char title in bold can still be cut.
🎯Put the primary keyword near the start of the title — it stays visible even when truncated.
📱Mobile viewport is narrower — always check the mobile tab if you target phone users.
✂️Google may rewrite your title if it's too short, keyword-stuffed, or doesn't match the page content.
📝Descriptions have no direct ranking impact but a well-written one improves CTR.
