How it works

✂️

Sentence splitting

Both texts are split into individual sentences, cleaned and normalised for accurate comparison.

🔢

Jaccard similarity

Each sentence pair is scored using word-overlap (Jaccard index) — no API or AI needed, runs entirely in your browser.

🎨

Colour-coded output

Sentences are highlighted by risk level so you can spot duplicated passages at a glance and fix them fast.

📊

Overall score

A weighted similarity score reflects what fraction of shared content exists between the two pieces.
